BPC-157

Body Protection Compound-157

Also known as: Bepecin

Low Risk
Healing & Recovery

A 15-amino acid partial sequence derived from human gastric juice. The most popular peptide in the wellness community for accelerating healing of tendons, ligaments, muscles, and gut tissue.

Benefits

Accelerates healing of tendons, ligaments, muscles, and bones
Promotes angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation)
Enhances collagen synthesis and fibroblast activity
Protects and heals gut lining
Anti-inflammatory properties
May support nerve regeneration

Side Effects & Risks

Nausea (mild)mild
Lightheadednessmild

Dosing Information

Typical Dose

250-500 mcg 1-2x daily

Administration

Cycle Length

4-8 weeks on, few weeks off

Notes

Also available as oral capsules with reduced bioavailability

Legal Status

FDA Status

Not FDA-approved for human use

Availability

Research chemical; some compounding pharmacies

WADA Status

Banned by WADA (athletes beware)

Evidence

Evidence LevelLow-Moderate

Extensive animal studies showing strong healing effects. Only 3 small human pilot studies (no RCTs). WADA prohibited since Jan 2022 under S0 (Non-Approved Substances). Most human evidence is preclinical.
